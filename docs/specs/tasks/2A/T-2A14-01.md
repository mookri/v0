## Objective

Minimal B2B structure lands on top of customers: `b2b_companies` group customer records (≤1 company per customer) with shared payment terms and buyer-user roles, and company-scoped tax exemption with uploaded certificates makes checkout skip tax computation via the tax engine's `exempt=true` context — every exempt order recording `tax_exemption_ref` for statutory audit. Company buyers check out with their company's payment term offered alongside gateway payment; prices remain base pricing.

## Scope

**In scope:**

- `b2b_companies`, `b2b_company_customers` (customer ≤1 company), `b2b_company_buyers` (role admin|buyer) entities
- Tax exemption: certificate upload (kernel Files, audited), checkout skip via tax-engine exempt context, `tax_exemption_ref` on exempt orders
- Exemption toggle as sensitive operation (`b2b:company:configure` + ACR 2)
- Checkout behavior: company payment term offered as a payment option (order_ops terms flow)

**Out of scope:**

- Company-specific price lists + negotiation flows — owned by Deferred (full B2B)
- Multi-company customers — owned by Prohibited by design (UQ)

## Acceptance criteria

1. **AC-T-2A14-01-1** — A customer belongs to at most one company (UQ on `b2b_company_customers.customer_id`); buyer users hold `admin|buyer` roles per company, and company records carry optional payment_term_id + tax exemption state.
2. **AC-T-2A14-01-2** — Tax exemption holds: GIVEN a company with `tax_exempt=true` and an uploaded certificate WHEN its orders pass the checkout tax step THEN the tax engine receives `exempt=true` context and returns zero tax with the reason recorded on the calculation snapshot; non-company customers and B2C flows are unaffected.
3. **AC-T-2A14-01-3** — Every exempt order records `tax_exemption_ref` for statutory audit (feeding return exports); toggling exemption requires `b2b:company:configure` with ACR 2 step-up and writes an audit record with the certificate file reference.
4. **AC-T-2A14-01-4** — Company buyers see their company's payment term offered as a payment option alongside gateway payment at checkout (order_ops terms flow); prices remain base pricing — no company price lists exist (ADR-0012 boundary).
5. **AC-T-2A14-01-5** — Events `b2b.company.created` / `.updated` / `.exemption_changed` are emitted with company references (certificate content never in payloads).
6. **AC-T-2A14-01-6** — GIVEN a principal lacking `b2b:company:view` / `b2b:company:create` / `b2b:company:configure` WHEN the corresponding request is issued THEN the platform returns 403 with envelope code `authorization_denied` and writes a denial audit record (negative path).
7. **AC-T-2A14-01-7** — Tenant isolation holds: every new table (`b2b_companies`, `b2b_company_customers`, `b2b_company_buyers`) is covered by the `AssertTenantIsolation` matrix — cross-tenant reads return no rows and unresolvable tenant contexts fail closed (enforced by the [Tenant Isolation Suite (standing)](../../../19-testing/testing-strategy.md#tenant-isolation-suite-standing)).
8. **AC-T-2A14-01-8** — The module manifest declares every permission, event, and navigation entry introduced by this task; `platform:sync-permissions` and `module:list` reflect them, and every state change introduced writes an audit record (DoD items 3 + 5).
