## Objective

Abandoned carts trigger a configurable recovery pipeline: the `checkout.cart.abandoned` event (session idle > 30 min) feeds a recovery listener evaluating store settings (`store.recovery`: enabled, delay schedule, max 2 sends per cart, quiet hours), sending recovery notifications with signed resume links through the notifications service. Guest carts require a captured email; buyer carts respect marketing opt-out (the restore link always remains in the buyer account saved-cart view); duplicate suppression is enforced per cart.

## Scope

**In scope:**

- Recovery listener consuming `checkout.cart.abandoned` with store-settings evaluation
- Delay schedule + send cap + quiet hours enforcement
- Signed resume links + conversion tracking; duplicate suppression via unique send-key in `notifications_log`
- Guest vs buyer consent rules; buyer saved-cart restore view

**Out of scope:**

- SMS/push channels (email channel v1) — owned by Deferred
- Discount incentives in recovery emails — owned by Deferred post-v1

## Acceptance criteria

1. **AC-T-2A10-01-1** — Recovery triggers only on `checkout.cart.abandoned` (idle > 30 min) and only when `store.recovery.enabled`; the delay schedule (e.g. [1h, 24h]) governs send timing with quiet hours respected, and no cart receives more than the configured max sends (2).
2. **AC-T-2A10-01-2** — Consent rules hold: guest carts recover only when an email was captured (addressing step reached); buyer carts with `marketing_opt_in=false` receive no recovery sends while the restore link remains available in the buyer account saved-cart view.
3. **AC-T-2A10-01-3** — Duplicate suppression: one recovery chain per cart id enforced by the unique send-key in `notifications_log`; re-abandonment after a restore starts a new chain still capped by max sends.
4. **AC-T-2A10-01-4** — Recovery sends and conversions are observable: the notification carries a signed resume link, and conversion tracking attributes the resulting order to the recovery chain (campaign tag in `notifications_log`).
5. **AC-T-2A10-01-5** — GIVEN a principal lacking `store:settings:configure` WHEN the corresponding request is issued THEN the platform returns 403 with envelope code `authorization_denied` and writes a denial audit record (negative path).
6. **AC-T-2A10-01-6** — The module manifest declares every permission, event, and navigation entry introduced by this task; `platform:sync-permissions` and `module:list` reflect them, and every state change introduced writes an audit record (DoD items 3 + 5).
